How it works
  • Your notes are always saved on this device first.
  • Backups go to your Telegram chat automatically: about 2 minutes after your last edit, when you leave the app, when the internet returns, and every 15 minutes.
  • Tap Backup now to send right away.
  • First time? Tap Telegram settings and follow the 4 steps.
  • To get your notes back, download the .zip files from Telegram and tap Restore.

Telegram

Backups are sent to a Telegram chat you control. Create a bot with @BotFather, copy its token, open the bot and press Start. For the chat ID, message @userinfobot, or use a channel name such as @mychannel.

The token and chat ID stay in this browser only.

Speech-to-text keys

Add keys from one or more speech-to-text providers. Requests rotate between your keys. If one reaches its limit or stops working, it rests for a while and the next key takes over. Keys are kept in this browser only and are not included in backups.
